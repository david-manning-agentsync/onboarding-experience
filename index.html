Onboarding Permission Sets

Assign one or more of the following Salesforce Permission Sets to grant users access to AgentSync Onboarding functionality.

Policy Admin
Create, edit, and delete Policy Sets. Configure GWBR rules, requirement groups, and compliance settings for your organization.
Firm Management
Manage carrier and agency account records. Oversee firm onboarding workflows, account status, and associated policy set assignments.
Invite Firms
Send onboarding invitations to agency and carrier partner firms. Initiate and track firm-level onboarding workflows from within Salesforce.
Invite Producers Agency only
Send onboarding invitations directly to individual producers. Available exclusively in the Agency context — carrier producer invitations are managed through Operating Manager flows.
Add Policy Set

Select one or more optional policy sets to apply. Only policy sets marked Optional on this account are available here.
